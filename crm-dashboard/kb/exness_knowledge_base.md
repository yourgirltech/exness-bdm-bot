## Leverage, margin and stop out explained

Leverage lets a trader control a larger position with a smaller deposit. Exness offers up to 1:Unlimited leverage on eligible accounts. Unlimited leverage is subject to conditions shown in the Personal Area (for example account equity limits and trading history), and higher leverage may be restricted around major news events, weekends and on certain instruments. High leverage magnifies both profits and losses.

Margin is the amount of equity locked to keep a position open. Margin level = equity ÷ used margin × 100%.
- Margin call: a warning level (60% on Standard, 30% on professional accounts). The trader should add funds or reduce positions.
- Stop out: 0% on all main accounts. At this level, positions start closing automatically, beginning with the least profitable one.
- Hedged margin 0%: fully hedged (opposite equal) positions require no additional margin.

Good practice for new traders: start with conservative leverage such as 1:100, use a stop-loss on every trade, keep position sizes small relative to the balance, and avoid risking more than about 2% of the account on a single trade.

## Market events and news briefings
